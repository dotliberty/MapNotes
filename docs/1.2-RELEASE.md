# MapNotes 1.2.0

Stable release approved by the owner on 8 November 2026 after the camera fixes. This promotes the approved beta.3 code without further feature changes.

## Changes

- Atlas-style action panels, typography and responsive layout.
- Isolated photos stay at their GPS anchors; only overlapping photo markers are spread, with stable identity and coincident-anchor selection.
- Manual front/back and available physical-camera selection, metadata-bounded zoom and preview pinch zoom.
- Corrected Camera2 TextureView orientation/aspect handling without applying sensor rotation or front mirroring twice; independent JPEG orientation.

## Scope and installation

Install the official signed APK over the existing official MapNotes installation; do not uninstall or clear its data. The separate `.debug` beta app is not the official app and is not updated by this release. Signing continuity does not prove a successful real-device update or migration.

Owner approval is not a claim of comprehensive physical testing: the full hardware camera/lens/orientation matrix, real GPS, Android 15 background behavior, accessibility and an in-place update with a real user's database were not independently exercised in this release run. Three skipped probes are not live-network acceptance. See [known limitations](KNOWN-LIMITATIONS.md).

The beta documents remain historical records; their unsigned/debug-artifact and unpublished-status statements describe those beta runs, not this stable release.
